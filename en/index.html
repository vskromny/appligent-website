Case 01 · Buchhaltung

Every Beleg lands in the books — and open questions become a list, not a pile.

Receipts arrive by email, photo, post and portal. By law they must be kept for ten years, on paper or electronically — and the Treuhand sector still handles 46 % of its mandates on paper. Source: OR Art. 958f ↗ Source: EXPERTsuisse / OBT 2023 ↗

  1. Collect One mailbox, one photo share and one Drive folder feed a single inbox. Nothing is retyped. E-Mail · Foto · Drive
  2. Extract & check Date, supplier, amount, MWST and account are read out and matched to your chart of accounts. Anything unclear is flagged, not guessed. OCR + Regeln
  3. Book & follow up Clean items are booked in bexio or Abacus with the original attached. Problem items go into a queue: contact → waiting → answered. bexio · Abacus

Case 02 · App production

A team of specialised agents takes an app from idea to the store. You approve twice.

An app normally passes through a strategist, a designer, developers, testers and a release manager — and waits at every handover. We replaced the waiting with a team of agents that hand over through state files. We found no Swiss statistic on stalled SME app projects that we would trust, so we print none; the numbers below were measured in our own pipeline, not in a vendor's benchmark.

  1. Validate & specify Idea validation checks demand, competition and store rules. A product spec follows — scope, screens, data, success criteria. Each stage writes its own state file, so the next role starts from facts, not memory. Idee → Spezifikation
  2. Design & build Design system and Figma handoff, then native code for iOS, Android or web — in parallel where the platforms allow. Code review and automated tests run on every change. Your first approval: the build. Figma → Xcode · Android Studio
  3. Ship & improve Release build, then submission to the App Store and Google Play — your second approval. After launch the team monitors crashes, reviews and ratings and feeds them into the next iteration. TestFlight · Play Console

Case 03 · Kreditoren

Supplier invoices arrive three ways. They leave as one approved payment run.

Paper QR-Rechnung, PDF by email, eBill — three channels, one spreadsheet. eBill alone passed four million users in June 2026, and the federal administration has required e-invoices above CHF 5,000 since 2016. Source: SIX 2026 ↗ Source: Bundesrat 2014 ↗

  1. Capture QR code, PDF or eBill is read into one list: supplier, IBAN, amount, due date, reference. QR · PDF · eBill
  2. Match & approve Each invoice is matched to its order or project. Only exceptions reach you — approved with one tap from email or chat. Bestellung ↔ Rechnung
  3. Pay & notify A pain.001 payment file goes to your e-banking on the due date. The supplier and your books get the payment notice automatically. pain.001 · Zahlungsavis

What changes

  • One payment run a week instead of invoices paid one by one.
  • Due dates are kept without anyone watching a calendar; Skonto is taken where it exists.
  • Suppliers stop calling to ask whether you paid — the notice leaves the moment the run does.

Case 04 · Debitoren

Late payers get a polite, punctual Mahnung — and you see who still owes what.

In the first half of 2024, 17.9 % of Swiss B2B invoices were paid late, on average 15.1 days overdue. Swiss companies surveyed by Intrum report 10.7 hours a week spent chasing open receivables. Source: Dun & Bradstreet 2024 ↗ Source: Intrum 2025 ↗

  1. Watch Open invoices are read from bexio or Abacus every day and matched against incoming camt.054 payments. camt.054
  2. Remind Day 1 overdue: a friendly reminder with the QR-Rechnung and a TWINT link. Day 14: the first Mahnung — in your tone, in DE or FR. You set the ladder; the law sets the 5 % Verzugszins. Erinnerung → Mahnung
  3. Escalate or close Payment received → closed automatically. No reaction after the last step → one line in your inbox with the file ready for Betreibung or Inkasso. Betreibung-ready

What changes

  • Every overdue invoice gets the same ladder on the same day — nothing slips because someone was on holiday.
  • You personally chase only the cases that reached the last step.
  • A live list: who owes what, since when, and what was already sent.

Case 05 · Steuern

The MWST return is ready for review on day one of the 60-day window.

Since 1 January 2025 every MWST-liable company files online via the ESTV ePortal — normally per quarter, within 60 days. 420,964 businesses are registered, 28 % of them on Saldosteuersatz. Source: ESTV ePortal ↗ Source: ESTV MWST-Statistik 2023 ↗

  1. Reconcile continuously Every booked Beleg carries its MWST code. Gaps — no MWST number, wrong rate, missing receipt — are flagged the week they occur, not at quarter-end. 8.1 · 2.6 % · Saldo
  2. Draft the return Turnover and Vorsteuer are compiled from the ledger and reconciled against revenue. Your Treuhänder gets a one-page check, not a pile. Umsatz ↔ Vorsteuer
  3. File & archive After your OK the return goes into the ePortal, the confirmation lands in the archive and the payment is scheduled for the due date. ESTV ePortal

What changes

  • Quarter-end becomes a review, not a hunt for missing Belege.
  • Deadline and payment are not missed — the system is the calendar.
  • Your Treuhänder reviews a reconciled draft instead of building one.

Is our data safe?+

Your data stays in your systems — bexio, Abacus, your mailbox, your Drive. Where an AI model reads documents, we name the provider and the processing location before we start, and use Swiss or EU processing where you require it. An NDA is signed before we see anything.

Do we need technical staff?+

No. We build, host, and maintain the system — your team only needs to use it. We train them until it sticks.

What does a first project cost?+

Most automation pilots are fixed-price and scoped in the first call. You know the number before anything starts — no surprises mid-project.

What if it doesn't work for us?+

We start with a small pilot against a measurable target. If it doesn't hit the target, you don't scale it — and you've lost weeks, not a year.
