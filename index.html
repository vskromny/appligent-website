FAQ

Questions we hear from every skeptical owner

Is our data safe?+

Yes. Your data stays in Switzerland or the EU where required, we work with your existing providers, and NDAs are standard before we see anything.

Do we need technical staff?+

No. We build, host, and maintain the system — your team only needs to use it. We train them until it sticks.

What does a first project cost?+

Most automation pilots are fixed-price and scoped in the first call. You know the number before anything starts — no surprises mid-project.

What if it doesn't work for us?+

We start with a small pilot against a measurable target. If it doesn't hit the target, you don't scale it — and you've lost weeks, not a year.
